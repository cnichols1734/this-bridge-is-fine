## Design Context

### Users
Curious drivers and civic-data people on a phone or laptop, often in a car-adjacent moment: “what am I driving over?” They are not engineers and not panicked. They want the file on a specific overpass, then a ranked list of the ones nearby that actually matter (traffic × condition), not a national dashboard.

### Brand Personality
Dry, civic, precise. The joke lives in the name; the interface does not wink. Quiet confidence, municipal instrument, slightly uneasy. Never “safe.” Never “about to fall.” Never campaign energy.

Three words: **dry, civic, precise.**

### Aesthetic Direction
Light mode. [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/new) on a map: white canvas, SF Pro, quiet chrome. McBroken’s bones (map + nearby list + worst-nearby) without leftover-blog thinness.

- Page `#ffffff`. Inset wells `#f5f5f7`. Hairlines `#d2d2d7`. Text `#1d1d1f` / `#6e6e73`
- No warm paper, no beige wash. Neutrals stay cool-neutral like Music, not iron-oxide
- Accent is one decisive red for Poor — not neon, not traffic-light green
- Christopher chose the original Good green / Fair gold for official G/F/P dots
- Type: **SF Pro Text / SF Pro Display** via `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI"`. No second display face. Tabular numbers for ratings and ADT
- OpenFreeMap Positron. Dots are the interface. Desktop click opens a map popup, not a side file. No pitch, no 3D, no teardrop pins
- Motion 180–220ms ease-out. No bounce, no confetti

Anti-references: Inter / Space Grotesk / IBM Plex / Overpass, warm off-white paper, oval pills, colored side rails, eyebrow labels, gradient heroes, blob icons over headings, card grids, “safe/danger” traffic-light toys, startup landing pages.

### Design Principles
1. **The map is the product.** Sidebars serve the map; they do not decorate it.
2. **One number you can repeat.** Area pulse and headlines carry ADT × condition, not raw ratings alone.
3. **A file, not a dump.** Detail shows the human sentence first; codes stay secondary.
4. **Hairlines, not chrome.** Full 1px borders or nothing. No left-stripe accents, no pills, no drop-shadow cards.
5. **Funny name, straight copy.** Precision over alarm.
